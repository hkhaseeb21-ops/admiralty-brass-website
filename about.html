Living with brass.

Dust, don't scrub

Wipe with a soft, dry cloth. Household cleaners and abrasive polish can dull the hand-finished surface.

Keep it dry

Brass is durable but not moisture-proof. Display it away from bathrooms and sinks.

Let it age

A light patina can develop over the years. Many collectors consider it part of the charm.
