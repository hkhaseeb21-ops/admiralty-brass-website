Common questions.

How do I order?

Add pieces to your cart on the shop page and send your order request with your shipping details. We then email you an invoice, and your order ships once it's paid.

How long does shipping take?

Orders ship from Ontario by Canada Post. Delivery time depends on where you live, and we confirm it on your invoice.

What if my piece arrives damaged?

Contact support with your order number and a photo. If a piece arrives damaged or not as described, we'll arrange a replacement or refund.

Can I get exact dimensions for a piece?

Yes. Email support with the name of the piece and we'll send you its height, width and depth.

Will mine look exactly like the photo?

Very close. Because every piece is hand-finished in small batches, minor differences in texture and patina are normal and part of the handmade character.

How do I care for brass?

Wipe with a soft, dry cloth and keep it away from moisture. Avoid household cleaners and abrasive polish.
