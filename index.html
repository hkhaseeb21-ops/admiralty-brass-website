Vision & Mission

Rooted in the Brass City.

Vision

Carrying a craft forward

To bring the centuries-old artistry of Moradabad, India — the "Brass City" renowned the world over for handcrafted brassware — into homes and collections everywhere, one hand-finished piece at a time.

Mission

Handcrafted at the source

Every hull, mast and fitting is hand-shaped by skilled artisans in Moradabad before each piece is finished, curated and shipped from Ontario, Canada — honouring tradition while building something new.
